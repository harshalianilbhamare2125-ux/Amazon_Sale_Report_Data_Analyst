### 🔍 Dashboard Features

**1. Sales by Category:** Analyzed top-selling categories like Kurta Sets (49.88%), Western Wear, Tops, Ethnic Dress, Bottom, etc.
**2. Geographical Analysis:** State-wise sales performance - Top states are Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh.
**3. Fulfilment Analysis:** 95.55% orders fulfilled by Merchant vs 4.45% by Amazon.
**4. Order Status:** Tracked Shipped, Cancelled, Pending, Shipped - Damaged / Lost in Transit orders.
**5. Monthly Trend:** Revenue trend by Month (April - March).
**6. Interactive Slicers:** Filter by Fulfilment (Amazon/Merchant) and Order Status.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
*   **Power BI Desktop:** Data Visualization & Dashboarding
*   **Power Query:** Data Cleaning & Transformation
*   **DAX:** For Calculated Measures (Sum of Amount, Sum of Qty)
*   **Data Modeling:** Relationship building

### 💡 Key Insights
- Merchant fulfilment dominates Amazon fulfilment.
- 'Set' and 'Kurta' category contributes to ~77% of total revenue.
- Maximum orders are in 'Shipped' status, need to reduce 'Cancelled' and 'Damaged' cases.
- Sales showed a downward trend from April to March.
